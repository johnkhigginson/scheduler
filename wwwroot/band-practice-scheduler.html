Schedule band practice around everyone's day job

A band is a small group with fixed costs: four or five people, a rehearsal room booked by the hour, and members who work shifts, teach lessons or play in other projects. The question is rarely which date. It is which evening this week or next, and what time the room should be booked from.

Set it up

  1. Pick the evenings. Under "Or repeat certain weekdays across a date range", choose Monday to Thursday and set the range to the next two weeks.
  2. Turn the evenings into slots. Under "What times?", offer time slots with a window of 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM and each slot 2 hours, which gives a 6 PM and an 8 PM start every evening. If your room rents in fixed blocks, set the slot length to match the block.
  3. Give weekends their own hours. Add a different window for Saturday, such as 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM. On Saturdays it replaces the evening hours.
  4. Drop the slots you cannot use. In the list of chosen days, tap a single slot to switch it off, such as the Tuesday at 8 PM when the room is already taken.

Quick for the band to answer

Members do not need an account. They type a name and tap yes or no on each slot. A shortcut line above the list sets an answer for a whole weekday or start time at once, so the guitarist who teaches every Wednesday can say no to all of them in one tap and then go through the rest.

Booking the room

With a band, the slot worth booking is usually the one where everyone said yes, and the tally under each column shows that. Once it is booked, "Add to calendar" gives everyone a calendar file, and closing voting from the organizer link stops late changes.

Scheduler picks dates rather than repeating slots. For a band that rehearses most weeks, a fresh poll every week or two takes a minute to make, and the home page keeps a list of the polls you made on that device.

Questions

A member's shift changed. Can they update their answer?

Yes. Their browser remembers their row, so they reopen the link on the same device and change it. Tapping the answer they already gave clears it.

We booked a gig on one of the dates. Can I take it out?

Yes. The organizer link lets you remove a date or slot. Any answers already given on it go with it, and the page warns you first.
