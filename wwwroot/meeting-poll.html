A free meeting poll for finding a time

When the people in a meeting do not share a calendar system, because they are clients, contractors or at different organizations, someone has to collect availability by hand. A meeting poll does it in one round: offer some slots, everyone marks the ones they can make, and the best one is highlighted.

Set it up

  1. Choose the days. "Weekdays next week" adds Monday to Friday in one tap, or click the days you want.
  2. Offer time slots. The window starts at 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in one hour slots. For a short call, set each slot to 30 minutes. Slots can be anything from 15 minutes to 4 hours.
  3. Take out what you cannot do. In the list of chosen days, tap a slot to switch it off, such as your standing lunch or another meeting.
  4. Add an agenda line in the details and a voting deadline, so the poll closes while there is still time to send the invite.

Time zones handled for you

The poll records the time zone you created it in. Each attendee sees every slot on their own clock by default, with a switch to show the times as you entered them, and the page always says which zone it is showing. Nobody has to work out what 2 PM Denver time means in London.

Answering many slots

A week of half hour slots is a lot of buttons, so attendees get a shortcut line that sets an answer for a whole weekday or a whole start time in one step, then adjust the exceptions. The slots are grouped in one card per day.

Sending the invite

The best slot is highlighted as answers arrive. "Add to calendar" downloads a standard calendar file for it, pinned to the right moment in the organizer's time zone, which most calendar apps can import. Send the invite from your own calendar as usual.

Scheduler does not connect to anyone's calendar, send reminders or book rooms. It finds the time, and that is all.

Questions

Do attendees need an account?

No. They type a name and answer. That includes people outside your organization.

Can I add a slot after I have shared the poll?

Yes. Open the poll with your organizer link and add a date with or without a time. Everyone's existing answers stay as they are.
