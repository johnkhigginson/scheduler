Pick the weekend for the bachelor party

Whoever is organizing has one job before anything gets booked: find a weekend the guest of honor and the core group can all make. It is usually two to four months before the wedding, and it has to dodge other weddings, work trips and the couple's own plans. The same goes for a bachelorette party.

Set it up

  1. Offer Fridays and Saturdays. Under "Or repeat certain weekdays across a date range", pick Friday and Saturday and set the range from now until a few weeks before the wedding. If it is all happening soon, the "Next 4 Fridays" preset adds them in one tap.
  2. Keep the dates all day. People are answering about a weekend, not a time.
  3. Switch on maybe for anyone still waiting to hear about time off.
  4. Put a rough plan in the details: the city and a ballpark cost, so people answer with the budget in mind rather than finding out later.
  5. Set a voting deadline before flights and lodging need booking.

Keeping it a surprise, if it is one

A poll is private by link. Poll pages are kept out of search engines, so only people you send the link to can find it. If the guest of honor is not meant to know the details yet, check their free weekends quietly with their partner, and leave them off the list.

Reading the result

Each date is answered on its own, so a Friday to Saturday trip needs both columns to score well. Look for the weekend where the tally is strong on both days. The highlighted best date is the strongest single day, and the guest of honor's row, or the answer you got from their partner, outweighs any count.

If you are planning with a co-organizer, give them the organizer link too. Anyone holding it can add dates, close voting or delete the poll.

Questions

Does this work for a bachelorette party?

Yes. The poll does not care what the party is called. Set it up the same way.

Can people say which weekend they would prefer?

Answers are yes, no and optionally maybe. For preferences, use the comments under the poll, which everyone with the link can read.
