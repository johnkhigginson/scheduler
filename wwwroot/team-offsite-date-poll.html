Find the days for your team offsite

An offsite only works when the whole team is in the room, and it usually runs over more than one day. People have customer commitments, school holidays and travel to check, and on a distributed team they are answering from different time zones. You need to see which run of days clears for everyone before anything gets booked.

Set it up

  1. Mark the candidate days. Click days on the calendar and shift click to add a whole week at once. If the offsite is soon, "Weekdays next week" fills in Monday to Friday in one tap.
  2. Keep the dates all day. An offsite is planned in days, not hours, and all day dates read the same in every office.
  3. Switch on maybe. "I would have to move something" is useful to know, and it counts for less than a clear yes.
  4. Use the details for the purpose, the likely location and how many days you are planning, so people answer with travel in mind.
  5. Set a voting deadline before you need to book rooms or travel.

Reading the result for a multi day block

Each day is its own column. A two day offsite needs two neighbouring columns where everyone said yes, and the tally under each column shows you that directly. The highlighted best date marks the strongest single day, which is a good place to start looking.

Comments are useful here too. "I can do that week, but not Friday, I fly home Thursday night" is easier to read in a sentence than in a grid.

What it does not do

Scheduler does not connect to anyone's calendar. Each person checks their own and answers. When you have picked, "Add to calendar" downloads a standard calendar file you can import or attach to the invite you send from your own calendar.

Questions

Do people outside our company need an account?

No one does. Colleagues, contractors and the venue contact can all answer with just a name.

Who can see the poll?

Anyone with the link can see the dates, every name and every answer. It is private by link, not by password, so share it the way you would share the agenda.
