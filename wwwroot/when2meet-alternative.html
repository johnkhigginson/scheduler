A When2meet alternative that is easy on phones

When2meet is free, needs no account, and has been a favourite for years. You drag across a grid of time blocks to paint your availability. That works on a laptop, but the drag grid is fiddly on phones, and the interface is showing its age. Scheduler asks a simpler question: here are some dates and times, can you make each one? Everyone taps yes or no.

What Scheduler does

  • Free, with no ads and no trackers
  • No sign-up to create a poll or to vote
  • Yes or no answers, with an optional "maybe"
  • All day dates or time slots, with different hours for different weekdays if you need them
  • An optional voting deadline
  • The best date highlights itself, and you can add it to your calendar
  • Comments on every poll, and answers people can change later from the same browser
  • Open source under the MIT license

When When2meet is the better choice

When2meet's grid suits a different question. If you have no candidate dates yet and want to see where free hours overlap across a whole week, and most people will answer on a laptop, its drag grid shows that at a glance. Scheduler works best once you have a shortlist of dates or time slots for people to say yes or no to.
