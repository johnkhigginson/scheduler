A meeting poll volunteers will actually answer

Volunteer groups are hard to schedule. Retirees are free on weekday mornings, parents after bedtime, people with jobs on Saturdays, and many of them will close the tab the moment a site asks for an email address. Nobody is paid to be there, so every extra step loses someone.

Ask for as little as possible

Scheduler asks a volunteer for one thing: a name. There is no account, no email address, no app and no cookie unless someone chooses to sign in. They open the link, tap yes or no on each option, and they are done. Scheduler never emails voters, so nobody ends up on a list.

Set it up for mixed availability

  1. Offer a mix of days. Under "Or repeat certain weekdays across a date range", pick a couple of weekday evenings and Saturday across the next two or three weeks.
  2. Give each kind of day its own hours. Offer time slots with a window of 7:00 PM to 8:30 PM and slots of 1 hour 30 for the evenings. Then add a different window for Saturday, such as 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, which replaces the evening hours on Saturdays.
  3. Switch on maybe, so people who could come if they find a sitter do not answer no.
  4. Use the details to say what the meeting is for and where it is, so people know whether it is one they need to attend.
  5. Set a voting deadline, so the poll closes itself and you are not chasing the last few replies.

Before you share it

Everyone with the link sees every name and answer. Tell volunteers a first name is fine. Then share the link wherever your group already talks: the newsletter, a group text, the noticeboard. The comments under the poll are a good place for "I cannot make any of these but can help set up".

If someone would rather not touch a phone at all, you can add their answer for them from your own device. Only the browser that added a row can change it later, and the organizer link can remove any row.

Questions

Is there a charge for nonprofits or community groups?

There is no charge for anyone. Scheduler is free, with no ads and no paid tier.

Will volunteers get emails from Scheduler?

No. Voters never give an email address, and Scheduler sends no newsletters or marketing email to anyone.
