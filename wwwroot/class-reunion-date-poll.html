Pick a date for your class reunion

A class reunion has a long lead time and a loose crowd. Classmates are spread across the country, half of them are reachable only through an old group page or a friend of a friend, and very few will create an account just to say which Saturday works. The committee needs a date before it can put down a deposit on a venue.

Set it up

  1. Start six to nine months out. Under "Or repeat certain weekdays across a date range", pick Saturday, and Friday as well if it is a two night event. Set the range across the months you are considering and add them all in one step.
  2. Leave the dates all day. Classmates in other time zones see the same dates you chose.
  3. Say which reunion it is in the title, such as "Lincoln High class of 2016, 10 year reunion". A link pasted into a group page shows that title in most chat apps and social sites, which is what makes people click.
  4. Switch on maybe, for people who need to sort out travel first.
  5. Set a voting deadline before the venue needs an answer.

Plan around the 60 person limit

A Scheduler poll holds up to 60 people. Plenty of reunions stay under that, but a big class can go well over. If you expect more replies than that, use the poll where it fits best: let the committee shortlist weekends among themselves, or ask friend groups to answer once for the group. Once the date is set, announce it in the same places you shared the link.

Share the link widely, and the organizer link narrowly

The poll link can go anywhere: the class group page, a mass text, a newsletter. Poll pages are kept out of search engines, so only people who have the link find it. The organizer link is different. Anyone holding it can close, change or delete the poll, so share it only with co-organizers you trust.

Questions

Do classmates have to give an email address?

No. They type a name and answer. Scheduler never asks voters for an email address and never emails them.

Can we add more weekends after the poll is out?

Yes. The organizer link lets you add or remove dates at any time. Removing a date also removes the answers given on it, and the page warns you before it does.
