A Framadate alternative with no email required

Framadate was a popular free date poll. The original Framadate service was shut down in April 2026, and the new version asks for an email address before you can create a poll. If you just want to send a link and collect answers, Scheduler is free, open source under the MIT license, and asks for nothing to get started.

What Scheduler does

  • Free, with no ads and no tracking
  • No sign-up to create a poll or to vote
  • Yes or no answers, with an optional "maybe"
  • All day dates or time slots, with different hours for different weekdays if you need them
  • An optional voting deadline
  • The best date highlights itself, and you can add it to your calendar
  • Comments on every poll, and answers people can change later from the same browser
  • Open source under the MIT license

When Framadate is the better choice

If your group already uses the new Framadate and nobody minds entering an email address, there is little reason to switch. Scheduler is a small personal project, and a poll tops out at 60 people, so it suits friends, teams and clubs rather than very large groups.
