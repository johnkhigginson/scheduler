A StrawPoll alternative made only for picking dates

StrawPoll is a general poll maker. It has many poll types, and one of them is a meeting poll where the options are dates and times. Scheduler does that one thing and nothing else, so the whole page is built around finding a date: a calendar to pick from, time slots made from opening hours, and a highlighted winner.

What a date poll needs that a general poll does not

  • Picking dates quickly. Click days on a calendar, shift click a run of them, or add every Tuesday and Thursday between two dates in one step. Presets cover the next 7 days, weekdays next week and the next 4 Fridays.
  • Time slots from opening hours. Say 6 PM to 10 PM in two hour slots and every chosen day gets them. A second window can give Saturdays different hours.
  • The right clock. Timed options show in each voter's own time zone, with a switch back to the organizer's. All day dates never move.
  • A clear result. Each yes counts two points and each maybe one. The best date highlights itself, ties are shown as ties, and the winner downloads as a calendar file.
  • A page with nothing else on it. No ads beside the poll, no analytics, and no third party scripts.

When StrawPoll is the better choice

If your group already uses StrawPoll for other questions, keeping the date poll there too is one less link to explain. StrawPoll can notify you when someone votes and send email invites, which Scheduler cannot. A Scheduler poll also holds up to 60 people, so a larger audience needs a tool built for crowds, and StrawPoll lists unlimited participants on every plan.
