A free Doodle alternative for group date polls

Doodle is the name most people know for finding a meeting date. If all you need is a group poll, Scheduler does that one job for free: pick some dates, share one link, everyone marks yes or no, and the best date highlights itself.

What Scheduler does

  • Free, with no ads and no tracking
  • No sign-up to create a poll or to vote
  • Yes or no answers, with an optional "maybe"
  • All day dates or time slots, with different hours for different weekdays if you need them
  • An optional voting deadline
  • The best date highlights itself, and you can add it to your calendar
  • Comments on every poll, and answers people can change later from the same browser
  • Open source under the MIT license

When Doodle is the better choice

Scheduler is deliberately small. It does group date polls and nothing else: no calendar sync, no booking pages, no team accounts. If you need any of that, or your workplace already pays for Doodle Pro and everyone knows how it works, stay with Doodle. A Scheduler poll also tops out at 60 people, so a very large group may need something else.
