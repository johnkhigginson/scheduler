Make a group date poll in a minute

Dinner with old friends, a weekend away, a birthday, a game night. The plan is easy. The date is the part that stalls when twelve people each reply with the days they cannot do. A date poll asks everyone the same question about the same dates, and the answer shows up on its own.

Create a free poll No account needed, no ads.

Three steps

  1. Pick dates. Click days on the calendar, or use a preset: the next 7 days, weekdays next week, or the next 4 Fridays.
  2. Share the link. Paste it in the group chat. Nobody needs an account or an app.
  3. Watch the best date appear. Each yes counts two points and each maybe one, and the top scoring date is highlighted as answers come in.

Tips for friend groups

  • Offer fewer dates than you think. Five to eight options get answered properly. Forty get skimmed.
  • Leave times out unless they matter. All day dates are quicker to answer. If you do need times, offer time slots and Scheduler builds them from a start and end time.
  • Decide about maybe. It is off by default, because a poll full of maybes decides nothing. Turn it on when people honestly cannot know yet, and turn it off again later if you like.
  • Close it when it is decided. The organizer link closes voting, so nobody adds a no after you have booked the table.

When there is a tie

Ties are shown as ties: every top scoring date is listed, and if lots of dates are level the page names the earliest. Use the comments to settle it, or pick the one that suits you. You organized it.

Questions

How many people can answer?

Up to 60 people per poll, choosing from up to 300 dates and times.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no paid tier and no limit on how many polls you make.

Do my friends need to download anything?

No. The poll is a web page, so it works in any browser on any phone or computer.
